What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow a website to recognize a browser or device and remember certain information about your visit.

PawStudio may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality and provide a smoother browsing experience.

02

How We Use Cookies

PawStudio may use cookies to help the website function properly, remember selected preferences, understand how visitors interact with our website, and improve website performance and usability.

The information collected through cookies may also help us identify areas where the website experience can be improved.

03

Types of Cookies

Depending on their purpose, cookies used on a website may generally be categorized as essential, preference, analytics, or similar functionality cookies.

The specific cookies or technologies used by PawStudio may change as website functionality and services evolve.

04

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies may be necessary for certain website functions to operate correctly. These technologies can support basic features, navigation, security, or other required functionality.

Disabling certain essential technologies may affect the availability or functionality of some parts of the website.

05

Preference Cookies

Preference cookies may allow the website to remember choices you make during your visit. These may include certain display or experience preferences.

Remembering these choices can help make future visits more convenient and consistent.

06

Analytics Cookies

Analytics technologies may help us understand how visitors use the PawStudio website, such as which pages are viewed, how visitors navigate through the website, and whether certain functionality is being used.

This information can help us understand website performance and improve the overall user experience.

07

Third-Party Technologies

Some website features or services may be provided through third-party platforms. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and practices.

PawStudio does not control the independent cookie practices of third-party services. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy and cookie policies where applicable.

Managing Cookies

Depending on your browser and device, you may be able to manage, block, or delete cookies through your browser settings.

You may choose to restrict certain cookies; however, doing so may affect the functionality, appearance, or availability of some parts of the PawStudio website.

09

Browser Settings

Most modern web browsers provide settings that allow you to view, manage, delete, or restrict cookies stored on your device.

The available controls and instructions may vary depending on the browser you use. Please refer to your browser provider's documentation for additional information.
